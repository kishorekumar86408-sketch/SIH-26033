## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution

We propose developing **FARM2HOME – A Direct Farmer-to-Consumer Digital Marketplace**, a web and mobile-based platform that connects farmers directly with consumers, retailers, supermarkets, and wholesalers without involving multiple intermediaries.

Farmers can register on the platform, upload their agricultural products, update available quantities, and set fair prices. Consumers can browse nearby farms, compare product prices, and purchase fresh agricultural products directly from farmers.

The platform will include AI-based demand prediction, real-time market price updates, secure online payments, location-based farmer discovery, and smart delivery management. It will also provide digital order tracking and transparent transactions.

By reducing unnecessary middlemen and commissions, this solution helps farmers earn more profit while allowing consumers to purchase fresh agricultural products at affordable prices. It also reduces food wastage, improves supply chain efficiency, and supports small-scale farmers.
## Technical Approach

1. **Frontend Development:** Develop a user-friendly web and mobile application using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React for farmers and consumers.

2. **Backend Development:** Use Python Django to manage user registration, product listings, orders, payments, and communication between farmers and consumers.

3. **Database Management:** Use MySQL to store farmer details, customer information, product details, pricing, orders, and transaction records.

4. **Farmer Registration and Product Listing:** Allow farmers to register, upload product images, enter product quantities, and update prices.

5. **Direct Marketplace:** Connect farmers directly with consumers, retailers, and wholesalers to reduce unnecessary intermediaries.

6. **AI-Based Demand Prediction:** Implement Machine Learning algorithms using Python and Scikit-learn to predict product demand based on historical sales and seasonal trends.

7. **Real-Time Price Updates:** Integrate market price information to help farmers make informed pricing decisions.

8. **Location-Based Services:** Use Google Maps API to identify nearby farmers and optimize product delivery routes.

9. **Secure Payment Integration:** Integrate UPI and other digital payment methods for safe and transparent transactions.

10. **Testing and Deployment:** Test the application for functionality, security, and performance, then deploy it on a cloud platform for public access.

**Expected Technical Outcome:** A scalable digital marketplace that enables direct agricultural trading, improves farmers' income, reduces consumer prices, and creates a transparent and efficient agricultural supply chain.

## Feasibility and Viability

### 1. Technical Feasibility
The proposed FARM2HOME platform can be developed using existing technologies such as Python Django, React, MySQL, and Google Maps API. AI and Machine Learning can be integrated for demand prediction and price analysis. These technologies are affordable, reliable, and scalable.

### 2. Economic Feasibility
The platform requires minimal initial investment compared to traditional agricultural supply chains. By eliminating unnecessary intermediaries, farmers can earn higher profits, while consumers can purchase products at lower prices. Digital payments and online transactions reduce operational costs.

### 3. Operational Feasibility
The application provides a simple and user-friendly interface that can be accessed by farmers and consumers through smartphones or computers. Local collection centres and delivery partners can help manage product transportation and distribution efficiently.

### 4. Social Feasibility
The solution supports small-scale farmers, improves rural livelihoods, promotes digital agriculture, and provides consumers with fresh and affordable agricultural products.

### 5. Market Viability
There is strong potential for a direct agricultural marketplace due to the increasing demand for fresh farm products and online shopping. The platform can expand to different villages, cities, and agricultural markets.

### 6. Scalability and Sustainability
The platform can gradually expand by adding more farmers, consumers, agricultural products, and delivery partners. Future improvements can include multilingual support, AI-based crop recommendations, weather updates, and blockchain-based supply chain transparency.

**Conclusion:** FARM2HOME is technically feasible, economically beneficial, operationally practical, and socially impactful. It offers a sustainable solution to reduce intermediaries, increase farmers' earnings, and provide affordable agricultural products to consumers.
